# Approved invoice PDF format — 5 November 2026

The PDF uses the approved clean white/navy layout, existing Streamline logo, top-right invoice number and current status, customer billing details, issue/supply/due dates and account type. Each booking contains its full route, collection date/window, service and applicable quote details followed by a Description / Qty / Rate (net) / Net table. Zero-value unused booking charge categories are omitted. Single-booking adjustments share that table; consolidated bookings are grouped individually with invoice-wide adjustments listed once. Long content paginates with repeated table headers and company footers.

Added charges, discounts and saved percentage adjustments are rendered with their saved net amounts. Displayed monetary rates are derived from net/quantity when a saved percentage is not a currency rate. Booking component displays reconcile to the saved booking invoice line, rather than changing its price. Totals remain the invoice's saved subtotal, VAT and total. Payments and issued credits appear when present; the amount due follows the existing settlement calculation. Paid invoices show Paid and £0 due. No booking prices, VAT calculation, account billing policy, invoice numbering, statuses, issue/due dates or financial records are modified.

Each invoice has a BACS reference consisting of INV- plus its unchanged unique invoice number. Invoice 01 therefore shows INV-01; invoice 02 shows INV-02. The reference remains stable when re-rendered, downloaded or emailed. A consolidated invoice has one reference for all bookings. Prefixing the complete invoice number also keeps legacy-format invoice references distinct. Bank/contact/company details come from the existing Company Info settings; no sample values are hard-coded into the renderer.

Only the invoice renderer, its regression tests and these release notes are included. The supplied Push-Approved-Change.ps1 validates baselines, backs up replaced files, applies them, builds the backend and runs the release suites before committing and pushing only manifest files. No database update or frontend change is needed. Once the backend deploys, invoice downloads and generated email attachments use the new renderer. Existing stored issued PDFs are retained as audit snapshots.

Validation: all 27 isolated regression suites passed, including six actual PDF rendering scenarios. Frontend and backend type checks passed. Actual PDFKit samples were generated and visually inspected for single Trade, consolidated Trade and paid Business invoices, plus mixed VAT/credit and long-content stress cases. Automated rendering checks verify expected charges/discounts/totals, stable references, all long instructions, page bounds and no mutation of input data. Typical single and paid samples fit one A4 page; the three-booking consolidated sample uses two pages. Sample PDFs contain fictional customer and bank details and are review examples only. No live invoices, payments, email or database records were changed during preparation.
